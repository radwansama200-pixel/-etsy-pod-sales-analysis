# Etsy Print-on-Demand Sales Analysis

Excel dashboard analysing 150 Etsy print-on-demand orders (April–June 2026) by category, country and product.

**Problem:** An Etsy seller needed to know which product categories and markets bring the most revenue, and where to focus next.

**Tools:** Excel (summary tables, charts)

**What I did:**

- Checked a 150-order dataset: date, product, category, country, quantity, unit price, revenue (no duplicates or missing values; revenue = quantity x price on every row)
- Calculated KPIs: total revenue, number of orders, average order value
- Built summary tables and charts for revenue by category and by country

**Results:**

- Total revenue $3,692.93 from 150 orders, average order value $24.62
- Hijabs are the top category ($1,081.40, 29.3%), followed closely by T-Shirts ($1,052.86, 28.5%)
- USA is the biggest market: $1,383.03 (37.5% of revenue), then UK (14.6%)
- Scarves have the highest average order value ($43.28), all from one product, Egyptian Pattern Scarf, which is also the best-selling product ($779.07)
- Digital Downloads are 24% of orders but only 12% of revenue (average order $11.96)
- Monthly revenue grew from $1,070 in April to $1,491 in June (+39%)

**Recommendation:** Focus on Hijabs and Scarves (highest value per order), push Scarves in the UK and Germany where they sell best, and bundle low-priced Digital Downloads to raise order value.

**Data:** Sample dataset (150 orders, 1 Apr - 29 Jun 2026)
